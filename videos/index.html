Before you start.

What do I get for $150 per week?

Five custom 30-second AI music videos, delivered over five working days, with two revisions total per week. That is $30 per video. This is a short-form weekly content plan, not a full-song video package.

Does the $5 preview count toward my weekly payment?

No. The $5 buys a separate 15-second watermarked preview at 480p. The weekly plan is $150 in addition to that preview. Buying a preview does not automatically enroll you in the weekly plan.

Are the two revisions per video?

No. Two revision requests are included in total across the five videos for the week. Tell us which clip and what you want changed.

Do you deliver seven days a week?

No. The plan covers five working days. We confirm your start date and delivery schedule with you before the week begins.

How do I join and pay?

Use “Request your weekly plan” to email us. We confirm availability, your start date and payment details before you pay. Online checkout for the new offer is being updated. Clicking a request button does not charge you or create a subscription.

What happens to my existing order or deposit?

Existing orders keep their agreed terms, including any deposit credit already promised. Use your existing order reference and the payment or balance link we sent you. The new separate $5 preview policy applies to new preview purchases for this offer.

Can more than one artist be in my video?

Yes. Each weekly video includes one artist. Duos, groups and featured artists receive a separate quote before production.

Can I choose an animal artist?

Yes. Send your song and animal idea. One character is included; extra characters are quoted separately.

Will these videos grow my music?

They give you more promotional content and opportunities to test different song sections and creative ideas. Results depend on your music, audience and posting strategy. Views, streams and sales are not guaranteed.

Can AI match me perfectly?

AI likeness, motion and lip-sync can vary. Clear references help us make a stronger result.
