Before you start.

How much does my video cost?

$1 per second, with a 30-second minimum. A 30-second video costs $30, a 60-second video costs $60 and a 180-second video costs $180. Use the calculator to choose your exact whole-second length.

Is the $5 preview included or credited?

The $5 preview payment (sometimes called a deposit to start) buys a separate 15-second watermarked preview at 480p. It does not reduce your finished video price. A 30-second video plus a preview costs $35 in total: $30 for the video and $5 for the preview.

Do I have to buy a preview?

No. You can request a finished video directly, or purchase only a $5 preview first. Buying a preview does not automatically order the finished video.

Can I choose a custom length?

Yes. Choose any whole-second length from 30 seconds. Use the slider or presets for common lengths, or type a longer length directly into the seconds field.

Is this a weekly subscription?

No. This offer is priced by video length, not by week. Request the video length you want, with no weekly subscription.

How do I order and pay?

Choose your seconds, then choose full payment or a $5 preview to start. Use the request button to email us your selection. We confirm availability and payment details before you pay. Checkout for the new offer is being updated; the request button does not charge you.

What happens to my existing order or deposit?

Existing orders keep their agreed terms, including any deposit credit already promised. Use your existing order reference and the payment or balance link we sent you. The separate $5 preview policy applies to new preview purchases for this offer.

Can more than one artist be in my video?

Yes. The base price includes one artist. Duos, groups and featured artists receive a separate quote before production.

Can I choose an animal artist?

Yes. Send your song and animal idea. One character is included; extra characters are quoted separately.

What about delivery timing and revisions?

We confirm timing and revision scope with your creative brief before payment. Existing orders retain their previously agreed delivery and revision terms.

Can AI match me perfectly?

AI likeness, motion and lip-sync can vary. Clear references help us make a stronger result.
