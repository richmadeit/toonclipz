Before you start.

Can I start with a $5 preview?

Yes. Choose “Start with $5” on any package, send your materials, then pay the deposit. Your $5 counts toward the total: $45 remains for 30 seconds, $85 for 1 minute or $245 for 3 minutes. The balance is due after preview approval, before we finish your video. You can also pay the full package price upfront.

How do I send my photo and song?

For a $5 preview, the next screen collects your materials before payment. Your song can be an audio file or a screen recording with sound, up to 50 MB. For photos, upload up to five JPG, PNG or WebP images, up to 10 MB each. A clear screenshot is fine. Choose your exact song section and keep your order reference to add more files later. If you pay in full, send your materials after checkout. Production starts after payment and materials are received.

What does my preview include?

A custom 15-second watermarked preview at 480p, made from your materials. Each package includes one artist, one outfit, one stage and one minor correction. New directions and extra elements are quoted separately.

When will I get my preview or video?

Your preview is ready the same day we receive your $5 deposit and all required materials. After you approve the preview, your full video is ready in 24–72 hours after the remaining payment and all required materials are received. For a full-payment order, that 24–72-hour window starts after full payment and all required materials are received. Final export quality is confirmed after we review your materials.

Can more than one artist be in my video?

Yes—duos, groups and featured artists are welcome on the same song. Package prices include one artist. Additional artists cost extra, with a custom quote based on your package length and scenes. Tell us the total number of artists and send clear references for each person. We confirm the extra cost with you before producing the group video.

Can I choose an animal artist?

Yes. For a $5 preview, send your song and animal idea first, then pay the deposit. A face photo is optional for an animal character. One character is included.

Can AI match me perfectly?

AI likeness, motion and lip-sync can vary. Clear references help us make a stronger result.
