Before you book.

Is this an entire music video?

Choose a short 15, 30 or 60-second promo, or the $300 full-video package for a song up to 3 minutes. The full video includes one artist, up to six scene concepts, one minor revision round and a 15-second teaser cut from the finished video. Longer songs cost $100 per additional minute, arranged separately by email before production.

What does a minor revision cover?

One round of small edits within the agreed direction, such as timing or shot adjustments, is included with the full-video package. New concepts, extra artists and additional revision rounds are quoted separately.

Can you create an animal character for me?

Yes. Choose Cinematic Animal Characters, then pick cat, dog, fox, bear, rabbit, another animal, or “Help me choose” in your order form. We create one character for your video; you do not need an existing character or a face photo. Upload your song and describe the outfit or personality you want. The Swingtails group is a portfolio example; additional characters are quoted separately.

What should I upload?

For a video of you, upload a clear face photo, your song audio, the exact start timestamp and a short creative idea. For a new animal artist, just send your song and animal choice; a photo is optional. Outfit references are welcome.

When will I get my video?

Your 15-second video is delivered by email within 24–72 hours after payment and receipt of your song, photo or character idea, and complete instructions. For longer packages, we confirm delivery timing by email. Have a release deadline? Email us before booking so we can confirm availability.

Can I see it before I pay?

Watch the portfolio examples before choosing. Custom previews and production begin after payment.

Can AI match me perfectly?

AI likeness, motion and lip-sync can vary. Clear references help us make a stronger result.
