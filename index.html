Before you start.

Is the entire video $5?

No. The $5 deposit starts your order and counts toward your total. Packages are $50 for 30 seconds, $90 for 1 minute and $250 for 3 minutes. The remaining balance is due after preview approval, before we finish the video.

Do I need my photo and song ready?

No. Pay the deposit first. You can send your photo, song and creative details afterward using your order reference. Preview production starts once we have your materials.

What does my preview include?

A custom 15-second watermarked preview at 480p, made from your materials. Each package includes one artist, one outfit, one stage and one minor correction. New directions and extra elements are quoted separately.

When will I get my video?

We confirm timing and final export quality after reviewing your materials. The remaining video is produced after preview approval and balance payment. Have a release deadline? Message @toon_clipz_ before paying.

Can more than one artist be in my video?

Yes—duos, groups and featured artists are welcome on the same song. Package prices include one artist. Additional artists cost extra, with a custom quote based on your package length and scenes. Tell us the total number of artists and send clear references for each person. We confirm the extra cost with you before producing the group video.

Can I choose an animal artist?

Yes. Send your song and animal idea after payment; a face photo is optional for an animal character. One character is included.

Can AI match me perfectly?

AI likeness, motion and lip-sync can vary. Clear references help us make a stronger result.
