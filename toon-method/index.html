Try the reaction-planning framework

Start with one moment from your own or permitted footage. This is a framework based on the finished example, not the original Tota generation prompt.

  1. Choose one exchange. Write the setup and payoff in one sentence. Keep enough context for the meaning to stay accurate.
  2. Lock the characters and setting. Make a consistent reference for each person. Choose flat 2D or cinematic 3D before generating.
  3. Plan a short sequence. For a 15-second idea, try 0–3 seconds for the premise, 3–10 for the exchange and 10–15 for a reaction or payoff. Adjust to the actual audio.
  4. Generate and edit around the source timing. Attach the appropriate character references and authorized audio. Keep who is speaking clear, preserve the meaning, and check expressions, identity and lip sync.
  5. Post, measure, repeat. Record watch time, completion, shares and follows. Test a related moment with a new payoff rather than repeating the same joke.

Starter style brief: “Flat 2D cartoon scene with clean dark outlines, simple cel shading and expressive adult characters. Keep each face, outfit and position consistent. Start with a medium group shot, then use one reaction close-up at the payoff. Keep the background simple and leave clear space for a short caption.”

Adapt the brief to your tool and approved references. Exact resemblance to the original and post performance are not guaranteed.
