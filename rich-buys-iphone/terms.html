What you are requesting

Internal eSIM-related repair parts identified by the supplier for the model families listed on the catalog. This is not an in-stock retail purchase or an official carrier-unlock service. Only experienced repair technicians and repair shops should request these parts.

A label match is not independent proof of compatibility. Rich must confirm the exact chip, region, carrier/software conditions, and matching supplier instructions before asking for payment. Work only on devices you own or are authorized to service.

Price and commitment

The minimum is 5 total chips. 5–9 chips are $15 each. 10–19 chips are $13 each. 20+ require a repair-shop quote. Shipping and any applicable tax are shown separately in your written quote.

A request does not reserve chips. Your commitment begins when Rich verifies full payment against your accepted quote. Cash App, Zelle, or another agreed payment method is handled privately. Do not send money to details posted by another group member.

Timing

The estimated 2–3 week supplier transit starts when Rich places the supplier order, not when you first message or pay. The open batch shows its request cutoff and planned supplier-order date. Your quote includes the date by which Rich expects to ship your package. Carrier transit to you is additional.

These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Rich must communicate a delay, give a revised estimate, and handle consent to wait or cancellation and any refund due. A note on a status page alone is not a substitute for notifying affected buyers. An unfilled batch is not permission to hold payment indefinitely.

Ask Rich through your private order page or the contact link. A cancellation request is recorded immediately; it does not itself move money. Rich processes any due refund through the payment method and records it in your order.
