What is collected

To review and fulfill a request, the site collects your name, contact number, repair-business details, selected chip models, postal code, and any notes you choose to provide. Rich adds the shipping address you confirm, payment method and transaction reference, quote, and order history. The site does not request card numbers, bank passwords, phone passwords, or customer IMEI numbers.

How it is used

Order information is used to review your request, contact you about the order, verify payment, fulfill the order, provide support, and keep necessary business records. This form is not an agreement to receive unrelated marketing.

The deployed service uses Netlify for hosting/functions, Supabase for order storage and owner authentication, and Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse protection. Their services may process technical request data, including IP addresses. Payment apps, Telegram, and carrier pages have their own privacy practices when you use them.

Your private link

Your private order link works like a password. Anyone you share it with can see the order's status, quote, model mix, and paid-buyer materials. It does not show your address, payment reference, contact number, or internal notes. Keep it out of public posts and group chats. Ask Rich to reset it if it leaks.

The site remembers only chip quantities in local storage. Your current order access key may be held in the tab's session storage so you can open the buyer pack. There are no ad pixels or marketing analytics installed in this version. The owner sign-in uses an HTTP-only session cookie.

Retention and requests

Order data is kept only as long as necessary for fulfillment, support, disputes, and applicable recordkeeping. Contact Rich to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to any retention obligations. Do not post these requests with personal information in the group.
