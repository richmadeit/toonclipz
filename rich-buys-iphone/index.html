QUICK ANSWERS

Know before you order.

Why do I need five chips?

Five is the starting order for this repair-business offer. You can mix models. The prompt pack and private buyer group are included after verified payment.

Do I need a storefront?

No. Experienced independent and side-repair techs may request 5–19 chips. Orders of 20 or more need repair-shop approval.

Does every listed chip work on every carrier?

No universal compatibility is promised. Exact model, region, carrier, software, and supplier instructions need to be checked before payment. These are not advertised as an official carrier unlock, Activation Lock removal, or a way to clear a stolen-device restriction.

Where is the installation video?

The 14 Pro / 14 Pro Max and 17 Pro Max chip cards have tutorial buttons. Each opens its matching supplier link in a new tab, not a video player here. The 14-series link matches the supplier QR labeled for both Pro models. Rich identified the other link for 17 Pro Max only. The videos still need review. Other models’ tutorials are not published yet. Check the exact model and instructions before installation.

Can I pay when the chips arrive?

Not for this preorder offer. Only paid, approved orders are committed. You see the full quote and date before paying.

What happens if the order is delayed?

Rich posts the update and contacts affected buyers about the revised date and cancellation/refund options. Your bonus does not remove your rights regarding the physical order. Use your private page or message Rich to request cancellation.
